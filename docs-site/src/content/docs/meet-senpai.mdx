Every dashboard needs one thing staring back at you while your net worth does whatever
it's doing today. That's **Senpai** — the small orbiting mark beside the dashboard. On a
desktop it settles into the lower corner. On a phone it occupies its own compact row after
the financial content, so commentary never sits on top of values or controls.

<img src="/FolioOrb/assets/shots/senpai-mobile-flow.webp" alt="Fictional FolioOrb demo data at phone width, with Senpai expanded in its own row below the World markets card instead of covering portfolio values" width="750" height="1624" loading="lazy" />

## Senpai has moods

Senpai's commentary changes depending on what's actually running underneath it:

- **Claude connected** — sharp, a little smug, and not shy about narrating exactly what
  your portfolio is doing
- **Local Intelligence only** — dry, matter-of-fact, entirely unbothered by the absence
  of an API key
- **Claude offline** — quietly sympathetic, acknowledging the outage without making it
  weird

## Interacting with Senpai

- Tap the orb to open or close the current line; tap an open line to cycle on demand
- Leave it alone and it will cycle on its own every so often. On a phone, automatic
  activity shows the compact indicator without opening the full line
- Hide it from the overflow menu's Intelligence section if you'd rather check your
  portfolio in peace

## What Senpai is not

Senpai doesn't manage your portfolio, doesn't place trades, and isn't a substitute for
the verdict engine or action plan. Senpai has *opinions* about your portfolio. Whether
those opinions are useful is between you and Senpai.
